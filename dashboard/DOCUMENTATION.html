1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

The Defense Dilemma: Modern Integrated Air Defense Systems (IADS) deploy dense multi-static, frequency-agile surveillance, acquisition, and fire-control radar networks paired with supersonic surface-to-air missile (SAM) interceptors. In high-intensity Anti-Access / Area-Denial (A2/AD) contested environments, penetrating hypersonic strike and reconnaissance platforms face three existential challenges that legacy avionics cannot overcome:

  • 1. The Reaction Latency Chasm: Human electronic support measures (ESM) operators and legacy analog EW pods require multiple seconds to detect, digitize, classify, and formulate jamming responses. At hypersonic speeds (Mach 5.2, or 1.77 km/s), a 3-second lag allows enemy fire-control radars to close more than 5.3 kilometers of uncountered tracking.
  • 2. The Decoupled Control Penalty: In traditional military aviation, flight guidance (navigation) and electronic warfare (countermeasures) operate as completely isolated, decoupled subsystems. Physical high-G evasive maneuvers without electronic deception bleed vital kinetic energy and cause severe aerodynamic heating spikes. Conversely, electronic jamming without coordinated aerodynamic maneuvering enables monopulse Track-Via-Missile (TVM) and Home-On-Jam (HOJ) interceptors to score lethal proximity hits.
  • 3. Severe Aerodynamic & Thermal Bounds: Cruising in the upper stratosphere (29,500 m) at Mach 5.2 imposes severe structural dynamic pressure limits, structural load bounds (G <= 12g peak), and aerodynamic frictional heating constraints governed by Sutton-Graves stagnation heat flux.

What COG-EW Solves: COG-EW solves this challenge by introducing a Cognitive Multi-Domain Co-Design Architecture that dynamically couples RF signal intelligence, automated jamming resource allocation, explicit RK4 aerospace flight guidance, and Gymnasium standard environments into a sub-millisecond closed-loop OODA (Observe-Orient-Decide-Act) cycle.

📌 CONTINUOUS 14-STATE VECTOR DEFINITION s(t) = [x, y, z, v_x, v_y, v_z, phi, theta, psi, p, q, r, m_fuel, T_skin]^T in R^14 Position: p = [x, y, z] in meters Velocity: v = [v_x, v_y, v_z] in m/s, V = sqrt(v_x^2 + v_y^2 + v_z^2) Attitude: Euler angles [phi, theta, psi] in radians (roll, pitch, yaw) Rates: Body angular velocities [p, q, r] in rad/s Mass: m_fuel remaining propellant (kg) Thermal: T_skin leading-edge stagnation temperature (Kelvin)

📌 EXPLICIT RK4 AEROSPACE INTEGRATOR k1 = f(t, s) k2 = f(t + Delta_t/2, s + Delta_t/2 * k1) k3 = f(t + Delta_t/2, s + Delta_t/2 * k2) k4 = f(t + Delta_t, s + Delta_t * k3) s(t + Delta_t) = s(t) + (Delta_t / 6) * (k1 + 2*k2 + 2*k3 + k4) Translational: dv/dt = (F_aero + T_thrust) / m_total + g(z) Rotational: d(omega)/dt = I^-1 * (tau_aero + tau_rcs - omega x (I * omega)) Propellant: dm_fuel/dt = -||T|| / (I_sp * g_0) Thermal: dT_skin/dt = (q_dot_stag - epsilon * sigma_sb * T_skin^4) / (c_p * rho_mat * d_skin)

5. Comprehensive Viva Defense Examination Questions & Answers

Q1: Is COG-EW a suicide drone or kamikaze weapon?
Answer: Absolutely not. COG-EW is an autonomous Hypersonic Reconnaissance and Standoff Strike Waverider cruising at Mach 5.2 in the upper stratosphere (29,500 m). Upon reaching terminal standoff range (within 15 km of the target bunker), it releases a dedicated hypersonic kinetic glide penetrator from its ventral weapons bay. The waverider never crashes; it initiates a supersonic climb (+10° AoA, ascending to 31,500m) and egress bank to safely exit contested airspace as a 100% reusable strategic asset.
Q2: Why co-design Electronic Warfare and Flight Guidance?
Answer: At Mach 5.2 (1.77 km/s), engagement windows last only tenths of a second. If electronic warfare operates without flight coordination, a high-G turn may expose the aircraft's maximum radar cross-section (RCS) specular flash directly toward enemy illuminators, or bleed vital kinetic energy into an unrecoverable stall. Conversely, jamming without flight maneuver allows monopulse tracking radars to guide interceptors to proximity detonation. COG-EW optimizes both domains concurrently.
Q3: How does Range Gate Pull-Off (RGPO) work physically?
Answer: The onboard jammer captures the enemy radar pulse, amplifies it (J/S > 18.5 dB), and retransmits it with progressive time delay (Delta_tau = 2 * Delta_R / c). The radar range tracking circuit latches onto the stronger phantom pulse. The jammer then pulls off at a_pull = 160 m/s^2, pulling the tracking gate beyond the range cell width and breaking missile guidance lock.
Q4: What is the Gymnasium Environment formulation?
Answer: CogEWEnv implements standard gymnasium.Env with a 28-dimensional continuous observation vector (kinematics, atmosphere, threat geometry, RF softmax probabilities) and a 4-dimensional action space (AoA command, bank angle command, EW mode selector, weapon release trigger), enabling reinforcement learning via PPO or SAC.
Q5: What caused the previous lag and how was it eliminated?
Answer: The simulation previously suffered from two compounding issues: (1) HTTP request pileup: setInterval ticked blindly every 100 ms without waiting for prior responses, queuing 20+ requests and blocking pause/reset buttons. This was fixed using an asynchronous isTicking concurrency lock and AbortController. (2) Garbage collection freezes: Three.js was instantiating and disposing geometries and materials every single frame (up to 200 allocations/sec). This was fixed by implementing a pre-allocated Particle Pool of 70 reusable meshes, stabilizing rendering at 60-120 FPS with 0 ms button latency.
Q6: How does the Sutton-Graves stagnation heating equation work?
Answer: The Sutton-Graves equation models convective heat transfer at hypersonic leading edges: q_dot = k_sg * sqrt(rho/R_n) * V^3. Because heating scales with the cube of velocity (V^3) and the square root of atmospheric density, cruising in the thin stratosphere (29,500m, rho = 0.018 rho_0) drastically reduces thermal loads compared to lower altitudes.
